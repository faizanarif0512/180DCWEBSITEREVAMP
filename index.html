180 Degrees Consulting offers high-quality consulting services without the premium price tag. Our team comprises carefully selected top-tier university students who volunteer their time because they share our vision and values. We partner with non-profits and social enterprises to provide innovative, practical, and sustainable solutions to the challenges they face.

Common Questions

We work with nonprofits, NGOs, social enterprises, government bodies, and impact-driven startups that create measurable social or environmental impact.
Our services are offered at zero cost or heavily subsidised rates, making professional-quality consulting accessible to limited-budget organisations.
A standard engagement runs 10–12 weeks per semester, staffed by 4–6 consultants led by a Project Manager.
Email 180dc@iitkgp.ac.in with a brief description of your organisation and challenge. We accept applications on a rolling basis before each semester.
